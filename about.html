About Us

KEY POINTS

Aeros Geotech Sdn Bhd is a company specialised in drones, located in Teluk Intan. Aeros Geotech Sdn Bhd was established in 2018 and distributes DJI Agras products. Their core business is in agriculture and mapping technology. They specialise in agricultural drone technology and services. In addition, the company has qualified DJI drone operators and trainers. Their core business is agricultural spraying and application services.

VISION

It is a proven fact that by utilizing drone technology, customers can significantly overcome labor shortage issues and reduce the risk to their employees when performing certain tasks that are normally categorized as ‘High Risk’ in Handling Pesticide Management. We assist our customers in their efforts to reduce their operating expenses by achieving the same results by utilizing drones to perform many tasks that are normally completed using more expensive options.

MISSION

Aeros Geotech Sdn. Bhd. mission is to provide Aerial Work services utilizing agriculture Drones to help our customers work safer, faster, and smarter. We help clients respond to emergency situations, perform preventative maintenance, and enhance the current knowledge of their existing assets.
